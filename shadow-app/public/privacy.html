SnappySjaak ("the application") is an internal operations tool used by [your company name] to manage freight, fust (returnable packaging) balances, and UK export documentation. This page describes how the application accesses and uses data from connected Google accounts, in line with Google API Services requirements.

Who this applies to

SnappySjaak is used only by staff of [your company name] and is not offered as a public product. Access is restricted to authorized employees.

What Google data the application accesses

  • Gmail — reads specific incoming emails and their attachments (shipment documents such as phytosanitary certificates, export files, and invoices) so they can be matched to the correct shipment record automatically, and sends outgoing emails (e.g. shipment-ready notifications) on the user's behalf.
  • Google Drive / Sheets — reads and writes shipment and stock data to designated spreadsheets and folders used for day-to-day operations.

How this data is used

Data accessed through Google APIs is used exclusively to operate SnappySjaak's internal shipment, documentation, and stock-tracking features. It is not used for advertising, and it is not sold, rented, or shared with any third party outside of what is required to run the application itself (for example, the email provider used to send notifications).

Data storage

Application data, including documents synced from Gmail/Drive, is stored in the application's own database and file storage, hosted on [hosting provider, e.g. Render]. Google account credentials (OAuth tokens) are stored only to maintain the connection between SnappySjaak and the authorized Google account, and are never displayed to or shared with other users.

Data retention and deletion

Synced documents and records are retained for as long as they are operationally relevant. A connected Google account's access can be revoked at any time from the account's Google security settings, which immediately stops SnappySjaak from accessing that account.
